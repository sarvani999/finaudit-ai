# ACME India Enterprise - Procurement & Financial Governance Policy

## Section 1: Purchase Order (PO) Mandate
- Any invoice exceeding ₹2,50,000.00 (Two Lakh Fifty Thousand INR) MUST reference an approved, active Purchase Order (PO) number.
- Invoices exceeding ₹2,50,000.00 submitted without a PO (Non-PO invoices) must be halted immediately for compliance review.

## Section 2: Delegation of Authority (DoA) Matrix
- Tier 1 (Manager Sign-off): Authorized up to ₹5,00,000.00.
- Tier 2 (Director Sign-off): Authorized between ₹5,00,000.00 and ₹50,00,000.00.
- Tier 3 (VP / CFO Sign-off): Mandatory for any single invoice or requisition exceeding ₹50,00,000.00 (Fifty Lakhs INR).

## Section 3: Vendor Tax & Payment Terms
- Standard corporate settlement terms are strictly Net 60.
- All invoices must contain a verified 15-digit GSTIN (Goods and Services Tax Identification Number).

# ACME Global - Enterprise Procurement & Financial Governance Policy

## Section 1: Purchase Order (PO) Mandate
- Any invoice exceeding $2,500.00 USD MUST reference an approved, active Purchase Order (PO) number.
- Invoices exceeding $2,500.00 submitted without a PO (Non-PO invoices) must be halted immediately for compliance review.

## Section 2: Delegation of Authority (DoA) Matrix
- Tier 1 (Manager Sign-off): Authorized up to $5,000.00.
- Tier 2 (Director Sign-off): Authorized between $5,000.00 and $50,000.00.
- Tier 3 (VP / C-Level Sign-off): Mandatory for any single invoice or expenditure exceeding $50,000.00.

## Section 3: Vendor Payment Terms
- Standard corporate settlement terms are strictly Net 60.
- Any invoice requesting accelerated settlement (Net 15 or Net 30) without an executed contract addendum violates policy.
